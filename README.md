BizPilot AI

Your Business. Your Co-Pilot.

An AI-powered business assistant for small and local shop owners in India.

About the Project

BizPilot AI is a multilingual AI assistant designed to help small business owners manage everyday business challenges with the power of Generative AI.

Our goal is to make AI-powered business support simple, accessible, and useful for local shop owners across India.

Key Features

- Smart Ad Studio: Generate product advertisements, headlines, captions, and promotional content.
- Festival Campaign Creator: Create marketing campaigns for Indian festivals and special occasions.
- Customer Reply Assistant: Generate helpful and polite responses to customer messages.
- Customer Simulator: Practise real-life customer conversations, bargaining, and sales communication.
- Smart Business Advisor: Get practical suggestions for business growth and everyday challenges.

Target Audience

Small and independent local shop owners across India.

Pilot Category

Clothing Shops

Languages

English, Hindi, and Bengali (initial testing).

Technology

- Amazon PartyRock
- Generative AI
- Prompt Engineering

Project Impact

Community Development

BizPilot AI aims to make AI tools more accessible to local businesses, helping shop owners improve their marketing, customer communication, and business confidence.
